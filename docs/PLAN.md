# Plan: Solar compute

> First draft, 2026-11-22. Every row is open for discussion in
> [Ideas](https://github.com/SoulCraft-Org/solar-compute/discussions/categories/ideas).

## Goal

Show how a sunlit data center can run AI compute with no net draw of power or water.

## Questions to answer

| # | Area | Question | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Where | On Earth, in orbit, or both, and what each one costs | ⬜ open |
| 2 | Energy | Panel area, storage and hours of sun for a steady load | ⬜ open |
| 3 | Neutral | Cooling with no water drawn from the town around it | ⬜ open |
| 4 | Hardware | GPUs per megawatt, and how the halls connect | ⬜ open |
| 5 | Cost | Cost per GPU-hour against today's data center | ⬜ open |
| 6 | Pilot | The smallest build worth running | ⬜ open |
